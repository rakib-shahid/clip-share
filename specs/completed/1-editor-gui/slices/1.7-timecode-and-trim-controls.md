# Slice 1.7: Timecode and trim controls

## Status

**Completed.**

## Contract

In and Out display `HH:MM:SS.mmm` and accept shorter unambiguous forms such as
`1:23.500`. Timeline and Arrow changes use 100 ms; Shift+Arrow uses one second.
Set In and Set Out copy the playhead through visibly button-shaped controls.
Reset trim restores the full source without changing audio. The selection is one
continuous range and remains the exact recipe sent to final rendering. Invalid
or too-short ranges show inline errors and disable finalization.

## Verification

Unit/component tests cover parsing, formatting, clamping, increments, 250 ms
minimum duration, reset independence, and boundary values at zero and duration.
