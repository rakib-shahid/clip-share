# Slice 1.12: Finalization warning and lock

## Status

**Completed.**

## Contract

Finalize proceeds directly only when the saved edit revision matches a ready
preview and no save is pending. Otherwise a nested warning offers Back, Render
preview, and warning-styled Finalize upload. Confirming snapshots and atomically
locks title, destination, recipe, and revision, then idempotently queues the final
job. Further edits/previews are rejected. The same locked revision returns its
existing job; a stale or different revision returns `409 edit_conflict`.

## Verification

HTTP/component tests cover all three warning actions, current-preview bypass,
pending saves, double submit, stale revisions, lock enforcement, and focus return.
