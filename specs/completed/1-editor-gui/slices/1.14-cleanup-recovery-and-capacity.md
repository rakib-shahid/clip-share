# Slice 1.14: Cleanup, recovery, and capacity

## Status

**Completed.**

## Contract

Capacity reserves source, final output, and one fixed 100 MB preview allowance.
Explicit discard, expiry, analysis/final failure, and cancellation idempotently
remove source, proxy, previews, recipes, descriptors, reservations, temporary
jobs, and hidden placeholder. Final ready commit removes editing provenance but
retains ordinary ready clip/job metadata and assets. Startup clears interrupted
preview locks/partials, preserves recent resumable sessions and last complete
previews, then applies the five-minute heartbeat cutoff. Final jobs use their own
one-hour deadline and outrank queued previews without preempting a running render.

## Verification

Recovery tests cover every terminal path, restart timing, capacity release,
priority ordering, and absence of orphan files or rows.
