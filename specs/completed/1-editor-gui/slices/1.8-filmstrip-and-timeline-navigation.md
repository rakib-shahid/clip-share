# Slice 1.8: Filmstrip and timeline navigation

## Status

**Completed.**

## Contract

One selection rectangle has draggable edge handles and a movable middle. Pointer
Events and pointer capture support mouse, touch, and pen. Wheel zoom centers on
the selection; Shift+wheel and horizontal trackpad gestures pan. During an edge
drag, scale freezes, the opposite edge stays fixed, and edge proximity pans the
viewport. Rezoom occurs after release. The scrollbar row always reserves height.
The last complete overscanned filmstrip remains visible until a debounced,
evenly distributed replacement is decoded and atomically swapped.

## Verification

Interaction tests cover outward drag, pointer capture, fixed-edge behavior,
zoom/pan gestures, stable layout height, and no blank or stretched frame strip.
