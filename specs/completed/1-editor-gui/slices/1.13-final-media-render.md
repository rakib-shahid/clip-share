# Slice 1.13: Final media render

## Status

**Completed.**

## Contract

Trim uses accurate re-encoding and zero-based timestamps. Compatible untrimmed
video may be remuxed for audio-only edits. Included audio streams receive their
gain, mix without normalization, pass a transparent -1 dBFS peak limiter, and
become one 128 kbps AAC-LC stereo stream; all-muted output has no audio stream.
Short audio pads with silence and long audio trims to authoritative video
duration. Output is fast-start H.264 High Profile, 8-bit `yuv420p`, at most
1080p60, with poster timing derived from trimmed duration and existing size
policy applied to trimmed duration.

## Verification

Processor tests assert duration/bounds, timestamps, streams, relative gain,
silence, poster position, fast-start, size accounting, and no publication after
failure.
