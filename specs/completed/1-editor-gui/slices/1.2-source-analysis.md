# Slice 1.2: Source analysis

## Status

**Completed.**

## Contract

FFprobe, not browser MIME or media metadata, establishes duration, dimensions,
frame rate, orientation, selected video stream, and audio stream descriptors.
Every usable audio stream records its index, codec, channels/layout, sample rate,
language, dispositions, and decodability. At most eight usable audio streams are
editable; nine or more returns `too_many_audio_tracks`. An unusable audio stream
is reported safely without rejecting otherwise valid video. Invalid or
undecodable video fails analysis and triggers complete temporary cleanup.

## Verification

Fixture tests cover silent, single-track, multitrack, surround, metadata-bearing,
unusable-audio, and nine-track sources. Responses expose no paths or FFmpeg
commands.
